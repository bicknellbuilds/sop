---
title: 6. Customer Property and Worksite Care
draft: false
---
All employees must treat client property with the utmost respect and care. Before beginning any job, inspect the work area and secure or remove any fragile items or items scheduled to be reused that might be damaged by work activities. This may include asking the client to move items, or safely moving them with the client. 

In the event of accidental damage, immediately report the incident to your supervisor and inform the client according to company procedures. Always ensure the work area is clean and returned to its original state before leaving the site. This means a thorough cleaning and a final inspection of the work area.
