title: 1. Jobsite Conduct
draft: false
---
While on the job site, employees must maintain a professional demeanor at all times. This includes no use of profane language, maintaining a tidy appearance, and limiting personal phone use to emergencies only. Smoking and vaping is not permitted on client property. Be courteous and respectful to all clients, their guests, and their properties.
