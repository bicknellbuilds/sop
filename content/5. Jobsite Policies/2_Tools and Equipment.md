---
title: 2. Tools and Equipment
draft: false
---
Employees are prohibited from any unauthorized use of the company's property, including its equipment, tools, machinery, or other equipment. Employees are responsible for the tools and equipment issued to them. 

Report any lost, stolen, or damaged equipment to your supervisor as soon as possible.
