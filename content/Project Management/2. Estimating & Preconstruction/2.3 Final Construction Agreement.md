Sales (with support from Estimating) to provide Client with Final Construction Agreement to define:

- Finalized scope of work, including
    
    - Defined work (including Default Selections Tier)
        
    - Contract documents list (with associated dates)
        
    - Undefined work (with target recommended 5-10% contingency)
        
    - Owner supplied materials/work (exclude responsibility & warranty)
        
    - Bicknell Excluded work
        
    - Remaining Client responsibilities
        
- Client Flow Chart (with project stage identified)
    
- Finalized Project Schedule (with Client/Others dependencies identified)
    
- Deposit Requirement & Payment Terms
    
- Construction Agreement (Terms & Conditions)
    
- Clear definition of time required from execution & payment to site mobilization (4 weeks minimum)
