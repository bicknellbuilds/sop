Before proceeding with preconstruction work, Sales to ensure:

- Preconstruction agreement is fully executed
    
- Initial payment is received
    

Sales to facilitate internal _Preconstruction Kick Off Meeting_:

- To include: President/Sales, Director of Operations, Estimator, Project Manager, Superintendent
    
- Meeting topics to include:
    
    - Project overview (Scope, Schedule, Budget)
        
    - Roles & responsibilities
        
    - Project risks & safety concerns
        

During preconstruction process, Estimating to ensure:

- Owner secures formal design completion & permit approval
    
- Site walk is performed to verify scope
    
- Subcontractor & material pricing is secured
    
- Long lead items are identified & monitored
    
- Final product selections are completed
    
- Pricing & schedule is updated & approved by Client based on permit revisions
    
- MEP coordination is complete
    
- Client responsibility matrix is updated
    
- Project schedule is finalized
