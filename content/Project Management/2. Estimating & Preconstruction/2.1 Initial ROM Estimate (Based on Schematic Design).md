Sales to provide Client with Pre-Construction agreement if project is still in development or if project will require several rounds of budgeting / estimating.

Sales to provide Client with ROM Proposal per project scope.

(For projects under $150,000 ROM, or is well developed (permit ready) skip to step 2.3).

Schematic Design ROM Proposal to include:

- Current scope definition
    
    - Defined work (including Default Selections Tier)
        
    - Undefined work (with target recommended 20% contingency)
        
    - Bicknell Excluded work
        
    - Client responsibilities
        
- Client Flow Chart (with project stage identified)
    
- Preconstruction Initiation Fee Requirement (with additive hourly rate) – Non-refundable $4,000 ($125/hour beyond 34 hours)
    
- Preconstruction Agreement (Terms & Conditions)
    
- Schematic project duration
