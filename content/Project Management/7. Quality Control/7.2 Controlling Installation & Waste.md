title: 7.2 Controlling Installation & Waste
draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure:

- Work is periodically inspected to confirm quality standard continues to be met
    
- Highly subjective/high risk work continues to receive periodic inspection by Client (& Designer), complete with documentation
    
- Subcontractors/Workers are notified immediately to cease work if quality standard is unacceptable
    
    - Best practices are used to minimize waste, while maximizing productivity, especially for long lead & costly materials
        

Project Manager to:

- Perform weekly Client meetings & site visits to ensure work is meeting contract requirements
    
    - Ensure all project records are accurate & recorded in a timely fashion
        
- Ensure all Change Orders are captured & executed in writing prior to performing extra work
    
- Monitor contract budget & discuss discrepancies immediately with Superintendent
    
- Discuss budget overages exceeding $5,000 immediately with Director of Operations
