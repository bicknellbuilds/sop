---
title: 7.3 Final Verification of Installed Assembly
draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure:

- Each assembly is reviewed by Client (& Designer) as they are completed to ensure acceptance, complete with documentation
    
- Completed work complies with Authorities Having Jurisdiction
    
- Exceptions/changes are documented & communicated to Project Manager
    
- Product Representatives are engaged when necessary to verify work is installed per manufacturer requirements, complete with documentation
