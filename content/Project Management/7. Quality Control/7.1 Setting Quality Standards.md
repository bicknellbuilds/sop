---
title: 7.1 Setting Quality Standards
draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure:

- Installed work & materials comply with contract documents & manufacturer requirements
    
- Installed work complies with Authorities Having Jurisdiction
    
- Identify & facilitate mockup construction to communicate quality standard with Client
    
- Work that is highly subjective or carries high risk is visually inspected by Client (& Designer), complete with documentation of inspection
