**Purpose**

To provide a standardized process to consistently deliver successful projects.

    

**Scope**

This procedure applies to all residential remodeling projects, including:

- Kitchens
    
- Bathrooms
    
- Additions
    
- Whole home remodels
    
- Basements
    
- Exterior renovations
    
- Specialty projects
    

**Roles & Responsibilities**

|   |   |
|---|---|
 
|**Role**|**Responsibilities**|
|President/Sales|Complete contract, collect deposit, transfer project information, high-level Client liaison|
|Director of Operations|Oversee project delivery, provide support to team, act as backstop for Client disputes & high-level risk|
|Estimator|Support project from schematic to construction phases, generate estimate, take-off & budget, draft project schedule, incorporate input from PM & Superintendent, order long lead materials|
|Project Manager|Manage project from preconstruction through completion, ensure Client expectations are formalized & achieved within budget, primary Client contact, responsible for project morale, cohesion & order|
|Superintendent|Manage site aspects of project from preconstruction through completion, ensure work is completed safely & meets contract quality requirements, primary site Client contact, responsible to flag any issues to Project Manager|
|Office Manager|Generate supporting project documentation, ensure financial & documentation controls are enforced, provide project support as requested to ensure clean project delivery, document deviations from SOP & recommend areas of improvement|
|Client|Responsible for design, general permitting, material selections, signing approvals, provide site access|
