title: 5.1 – Required Permits & Approvals
draft: false
---
Superintendent to verify all required permits are secured to include:

- Demo permit
    
- Building permit
    
- Electrical, Plumbing, Mechanical permits
    
- HOA approval (if applicable)
    
- Historic district approval (if applicable)
    
- Engineering approval (if applicable)
    

Superintendent to ensure all required inspections are incorporated into project schedule
