title: 5.2 Material Procurement
draft: false
---
Project Manager to ensure all approved materials are ordered and are:

- Approved by Client
    
- In procurement with delivery dates confirmed (or readily available)
    
- Any exceptions should be tracked with Superintendent & communicated to Client on weekly basis to include projected schedule impact
