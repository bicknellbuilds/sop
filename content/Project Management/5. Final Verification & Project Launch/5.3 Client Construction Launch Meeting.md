Project Manager (with Superintendent support) to conduct on-site meeting with Client (and designers if applicable) to include:

- Current Project Schedule (Including outstanding project selections & delays)
    
- Project Logistics: (Emergency Contact Info, Working hours/days, Parking, Access, Dumpster, Restroom Usage)
    
- Personal Site Protection: (Pet management, existing finishes & belongings, dust control)
    
- Project Controls: (Communication expectations, Reporting, Change Order process, Payment schedule, Notice of Intent to Lien)
    
    - 1 Business Day response time
        
    - Change Orders in writing
        
    - Daily & Weekly Reporting Functions
        
    

Answer all Client questions before ending meeting

Project Manager to distribute Meeting Minutes to Client to memorialize expectations
