PM to notify Client (with Superintendent support) when:

- Any site/existing conditions impact contract schedule
    
- Unforeseen conditions are discovered
    
- Missing/late material selections will impact schedule
    

Superintendent to document & notify Project Manager immediately

Ensure Clients & Client Agents participate in site safety orientation process
