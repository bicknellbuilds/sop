---
title: 6.3 Trade Management
draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure Subcontractors & Workers:

- Mobilize site as needed to achieve contract dates
    
- Participate in site safety orientation process
    
- Provide & comply with Site Specific Safety Plan (or comply with Bicknell SSSP as appropriate)
    
- Protect existing finishes surrounding work areas & keep site clutter free
    
- Notify Superintendent immediately regarding any unsafe or newly discovered condition
