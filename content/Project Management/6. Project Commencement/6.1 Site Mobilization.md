draft: false
---
Superintendent to direct site mobilization to include:

- Direct notification of Client one workday (24 hour minimum) before mobilization
    
- Initial tool & material delivery
    
- Security considerations (fencing, cameras, lighting, locks & chains, etc)
    
- Mobile office, job trailer, dumpster, portable toilet as required
    
- Job binder complete with project info, safety documentation and fillable forms
