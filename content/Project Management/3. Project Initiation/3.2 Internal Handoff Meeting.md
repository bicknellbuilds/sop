title: 3.2 Internal Handoff Meeting
draft: false
---
Within 3 business days of contract signing, Sales to facilitate _Construction Handoff Meeting_.

- To include: President/Sales, Director of Operations, Estimator, Project Manager, Superintendent
    
- Meeting topics to include:
    
    - Project overview (Scope, Schedule, Budget)
        
    - Roles & responsibilities
        
    - Project risks & safety concerns
        
    - Customer Expectations
        

- Discuss special requests
    
- Allowances
    
- Long lead items
