Project Manager to verify the following documents are complete:

- Executed contract
    
- Approved plans & Specifications
    
- Finish selections (Including Default Selections Tier)
    
- Site photos
    
- Existing conditions
    
- Measurements
    
- Engineering (if required)
    
- Structural drawings
    
- HOA requirements (if applicable)
