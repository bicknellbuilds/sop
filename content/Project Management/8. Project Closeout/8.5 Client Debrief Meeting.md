Sales/Project Manager to hold in-person meeting with Client following project completion to include:

- Sincere gratitude for opportunity to complete Client project
    
- Formal transfer of final project documentation with explanation of contents including:
    
    - Project photos flash drive as a gift (with request to notify Bicknell if Client takes exception to public use/sharing of photos for marketing purposes)
        
    - Notice of Warranty Walk at 11 months
        
    - Presentation of Client appreciation gift (if applicable)
        
    
- Request for specific feedback on project highlights & opportunities to improve
    
    - Presentation of Google “Five Star Club” Review Card and request for public review
