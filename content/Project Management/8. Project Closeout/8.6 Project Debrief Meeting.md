draft: false
---
Project Manager to facilitate _Project Debrief Meeting_ for projects over $150k to include Bicknell team, to review:

- Project Bottom Line
    
    - Scopes significantly over or under budget & associated reasons
        
    - Estimating misses & lessons learned
        
    - Scheduling delays & found efficiencies
        
    
    - Project Stakeholders & Partners
        
        - Client feedback for Bicknell
            
        - Team assessment of Client
            
        - Team assessment of project partners (Designers, Subcontractors, etc)
            
    - Bicknell Review
        
        - Process review and areas of improvement
            
        - Team MVP
            
        - Action Items
