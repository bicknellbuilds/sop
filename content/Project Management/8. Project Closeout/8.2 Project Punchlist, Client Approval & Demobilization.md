title: 8.2 Project Punchlist, Client Approval & Demobilization
draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure:

- Final Client (& Designer) punchlist is generated
    
- Project Manager is notified immediately, prior to completing work for punchlist items that are extra/changed work
    
- Subcontractors/Workers are directed to complete punchlist
    
- Any delays related to completing punchlist/approved added work are documented & sent to Project Manager
    
- Contracted attic stock is left stored in Client requested location, complete with documentation
    
- Project is demobilized as appropriate following completed work & final acceptance from Client
    
- Project photos are captured (by Bicknell or 3rd party) & delivered to Office Manager
    
- Project binder is delivered to Office Manager
    

Project Manager to ensure:

- Client receives final punchlist, complete with Change Orders for requested extra/changed work
    
- Superintendent is notified immediately when Change Orders are approved by Client
