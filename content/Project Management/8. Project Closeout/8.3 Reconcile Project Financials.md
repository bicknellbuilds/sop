draft: false
---
Superintendent to ensure:

- All final documentation, invoices & time cards are delivered to Office Manager
    

Project Manager to ensure:

- All pending Change Orders (& claims) are approved & closed out with Client
    
- All project costs have been realized, completed by notifying Office Manager
    

Office Manager to ensure:

- All pending project costs are closed
    
- Final Client payment is received
    
- Any discrepancies are communicated to Project Manager immediately
    
- Final profitability report is provided to Project Manager & Superintendent
