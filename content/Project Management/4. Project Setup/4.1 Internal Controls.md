The Office Manager shall verify all project files are created:

- Buildbook project setup
    
- Digital job file (Including Safety Documents)
    
- Jobsite Binder (and Safety Forms included)
    
- Accounting job number
    
- Contract budget entered into QB
    
- Insurance documentation
    
- Procurement tracking setup
    
- Client contact info (& emergency contact) verified
    
- Notice of Intent to Lien
    
- Closeout/Warranty file
