---
title: 4.2 Site Verification
draft: false
---
The Superintendent to perform site visit to verify:

- Critical measurements are correct
    
- Existing conditions are documented via photo/video
    
- Utilities & shut-offs are located
    
- Personnel & delivery access points
    
- Homeowner concerns
    
- Unforeseen conditions are captured
    

Any discrepancies must be documented & resolved before construction begins.
