**1.3 Initial Site Walk**

Perform site walk, if possible, Sales to verify:

- Existing conditions of project & property (capture photo/video with Client permission)
    
- Site access requirements (to include haul in/out & parking)
    
- Project dimensions
    
- Potential safety hazards
    
- Excluded work/limits of work
    
- Utility conditions & access requirements
    
- Personal considerations (occupants, children, pets, neighbors, special requirements)
