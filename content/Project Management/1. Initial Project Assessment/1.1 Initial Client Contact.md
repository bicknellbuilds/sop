During initial Client contact, Sales to verify:

- Approximate scope & scale of requested work
    
- Desired project timeline, if applicable
    
- Budget boundaries and financing
    
- Current stage of project design & permitting (Refer to _Client Flow Chart_)
    
- Current list of engaged design professionals
    
- Client is provided with a draft Preconstruction Agreement, if relevant
