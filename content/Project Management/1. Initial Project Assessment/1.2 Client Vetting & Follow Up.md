- Primary client contact is legal owner of project property ([per WA State Recorders Office website](https://kingcounty.gov/en/dept/executive-services/certificates-permits-licenses/records-licensing/recorders-office/records-search))
    
- Location & neighborhood of project (per Google Maps)
    
- Social media presence of Client contact/property owner
    
    - May include Facebook, LinkedIn, Yelp, Google Reviews, etc
