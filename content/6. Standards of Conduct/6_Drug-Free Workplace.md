---
title: 6. Drug-Free Workplace
draft: false
---
Bicknell Builds employees are responsible for helping maintain a safe and healthy work environment. Employees abusing drugs and alcohol are less productive and are a risk to the safety and productivity of our company. Therefore, the company prohibits the possession, manufacture, use, and distribution of all controlled substances in the workplace or at times that may impact your work performance.
