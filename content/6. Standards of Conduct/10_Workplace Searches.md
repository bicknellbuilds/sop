---
title: 10. Workplace Searches
draft: false
---
The company prioritizes the safety, protection, and well-being of employees and its business interests. The company reserves the right to search all company property for all business purposes, including compliance with company policies, protecting the company's rights, property, and interests, investigating breaches of security, and investigating possible violations of the law. Employees are expected to cooperate in the conduct of any search or inspection.
