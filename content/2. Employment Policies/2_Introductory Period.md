---
title: 2. Introductory Period
draft: false
---
The first 90 days of employment are an introductory period. This is an opportunity for Bicknell Builds to evaluate your performance and suitability for the role. It is also an opportunity for you to decide whether you are happy being employed at Bicknell Builds. The company may extend the introductory period if it desires. If at the end of the introductory period, the relationship is satisfactory to the new employee and the manager, the employment relationship will continue.
