Compass Statement: 

Relationships are the 'True North' of Bicknell Builds. Our relationships are everything to us, and we put people first. We do our work in ways that inspire trust and connection in our customers, our trade partners and our own team. 

Code of Honor: 

Promise 1: 

At Bicknell Builds, you can count on us to deliver what we say we will. We take pride in building and renovating homes through collaboration, transparency and our deep breadth of expertise. We strive to bring our customers peace of mind and an easy-to-navigate process in bringing your vision to life. 

Promise 2 

At Bicknell Builds, we strive to build a culture that emphasizes good intent, recognition that we are all on the same team, and supporting one another through whatever comes. We commit to: 

• Provide a clearly defined, open and honest environment for our team 

• Invest in growth for the company and for every team member  

• Foster a culture of trust and respect, and 

• Focus on safety to keep everyone going home in one piece. 

Promise 3 

At Bicknell Builds, we believe in teams that collaborate with honesty and respect where every member brings their best to the table, every day. In that interest, we each promise: 

• To tell each other the truth, even when it's tough 

• To treat each other with compassion and respect 

• To lean in, bringing our best to every challenge 

• To work to evolve every day, in terms of our skills, our relationships and ourselves.
