Safety Protocols for On-Site Work Policy 

Employees are required to follow all safety protocols strictly as outlined in the Bicknell Builds Accident Prevention Program, including the use of personal protective equipment (PPE) relevant to the work at hand.  

PPE Requirements: 

Before starting work, assess the necessary PPE for the specific task. Use only PPE that meets industry standards and is approved by the company. 

Stop-work Authority 

Every employee has authority to stop work when unsafe conditions exist. 

Employees must: 

1. Stop affected work. 
    
2. Notify supervision. 
    
3. Remain clear of hazards. 
    

No retaliation will occur for good-faith safety concerns.
