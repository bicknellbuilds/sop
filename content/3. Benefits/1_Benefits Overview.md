This handbook contains descriptions of some of our current employee benefits. Many of the company’s benefit plans are described in more formal plan documents available with your specific hiring package. In the event of any inconsistencies between this handbook or any other oral or written description of benefits and a formal plan document, the formal plan document will govern. The information presented here is intended to serve only as an overview.
